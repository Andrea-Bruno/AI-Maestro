Profiles — Your Roast Library

The Profiles panel is where you manage all the roasts you have saved. Every time you end a roast, it becomes a profile — a complete record with temperatures, times, events, and notes. Think of it as a library of every roast you have ever done.

Profile List

When you open the Profiles tab, you see a list of all saved profiles. Click a profile name to view it. The refresh button reloads the list from the server (useful if another computer saved a new profile).

From the list you can:

ActionHow to do it
View a profileClick its name. It loads into the Analysis panel where you can see all the numbers.
Delete a profileOnly in Full mode. Click the X button next to the profile name to remove it permanently.

Target Curve Designer

Before you start a roast, you can design an ideal temperature curve. Enter four key points and Maestro AI draws a reference line that you can follow during the roast.

What to enterWhat it means
Charge TempThe temperature of the drum when you load the coffee.
Dry EndThe temperature you want at the end of the drying phase (beans turn from green to yellow, around 160°C).
FC StartThe temperature where you want First Crack to happen (usually around 190–200°C).
DropThe temperature at which you plan to take the beans out.

Press Create Target and the reference curve appears on the chart during your roast as a purple dotted line.

Phase Ranges

You can tell Maestro AI what temperatures mark the start of each phase. This helps the system correctly identify when the beans enter Drying, Maillard, and Development phases. If your machine runs hot or cold, adjusting these ranges makes the phase detection more accurate.

Properties

When you load a profile, you can edit its properties: weight in and weight out (to calculate loss), and the whole-bean color (Agtron value). These numbers are saved with the profile and used in the Analysis panel.

Transformer

Sometimes you have a profile that was made with a different batch size or machine, and you want to adapt it. The Transformer lets you:

OperationWhat it does
Time ScaleStretch or shrink the time axis. Useful if the original roast was 10 minutes and yours needs to be 12.
Temp OffsetShift all temperatures up or down by a fixed amount. Useful if your machine runs 5°C hotter than the original.
InvertFlip the curve upside down (rarely used, but handy for special analyses).
C → FConvert all temperatures from Celsius to Fahrenheit.

Import and Export

You can bring profiles in from other programs or share your profiles with others.

To import a profile:

  • Drag and drop a .maestro, .alog (Artisan), or .json file onto the drop zone.
  • Or click the Import button to select a file from your computer.
  • Or paste the file content into the text area.

To export a profile: pick a format (JSON or .alog), select which profile to export, and press Export. Use this to back up your profiles or share them with another roaster.

Artisan profiles (.alog) are automatically converted when imported — all events and temperature data are preserved.

Digital Signing

In Full mode, you can sign your profiles with a digital signature. This guarantees that the profile was created by you and has not been tampered with. You need a private key to sign, and anyone with your public key can verify the signature. Useful for competitions or when sharing profiles professionally.
