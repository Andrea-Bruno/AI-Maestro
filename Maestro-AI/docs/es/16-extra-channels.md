# Extra Channels

Monitor additional temperature points beyond the standard BT/ET pair.

## Usage

Extra channels let you connect additional thermocouples for monitoring:
- Exhaust gas temperature
- Drum surface temperature
- Bean trier temperature
- Afterburner temperature

Each channel has its own BT/ET input and appears in the Extra Channels Graph below the main chart.

## Adding Channels

1. Click **+ Add Ch** to add a new channel
2. Enter a **Name** (e.g., "Exhaust", "Drum")
3. Enter **BT** and **ET** temperature values
4. Click **+** to record the reading
5. Click **✕** to remove a channel

Channels are unlimited — add as many as your hardware supports.

## Chart

Extra channels appear in a separate ECharts graph with distinct colors for each channel.
